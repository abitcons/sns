Elm and SNS: Advancing Digital Transformation in Saudi Arabia

Understand the verified relationship between Elm and Smart National Solutions and what it means for enterprise digital transformation in Saudi Arabia.

By Smart National Solutions · September 30, 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Elm first announced an investment in SNS to support growth and enterprise digital services.
  • Elm announced a preliminary move toward 40 percent ownership in February 2025.
  • Elm reports that ownership increased from 24 percent to 40 percent during 2025 and that joint control was established.
  • SNS continues to provide systems integration, SAP, AI, data, software, and enterprise support capabilities.

Quick answer

Elm is a significant investor in Smart National Solutions. Elm's 2025 annual report states that its ownership in SNS increased from 24 percent to 40 percent during 2025 and that joint control over key strategic and operational decisions was established. The report classifies the investment as a joint venture. SNS continues to deliver enterprise technology, SAP, integration, AI, data, and software services from Saudi Arabia.

Who are Elm and Smart National Solutions?

Elm is a Saudi digital solutions company serving government and enterprise markets. Smart National Solutions, commonly known as SNS, is a Riyadh-based technology and consulting company specializing in systems integration, SAP solutions, AI and data, custom software, cloud services, and enterprise support.

The organizations have complementary positions in Saudi Arabia's digital economy. Elm brings scale and a broad digital-services portfolio, while SNS brings enterprise implementation capability, SAP expertise, local intellectual property, and specialized delivery teams.

A verified timeline of the Elm and SNS relationship

Initial investment

Elm announced its investment in Smart National Solutions as part of a strategy to support promising technology companies. In its announcement, Elm described SNS as a provider of resource-management systems, enterprise application integration, and data services. SNS CEO Abdulmajeed Almousa highlighted the company's consulting, business integration, SAP enterprise solutions, and business-intelligence capabilities.

Expansion announced at LEAP 2025

On February 12, 2025, Elm announced a preliminary term sheet to increase its ownership in SNS to 40 percent. The announcement said the move was intended to facilitate integration between the companies' digital solutions and support Saudi Arabia's digital transformation. Elm also identified SNS experience with government entities, PIF-related companies, and major private-sector organizations.

Ownership increase reported for 2025

Elm's 2025 annual report states that its ownership in Smart National Solutions increased from 24 percent to 40 percent. It also states that a new shareholders' agreement established joint control over key strategic and operational decisions, leading Elm to reclassify the investment from an associate to a joint venture for reporting purposes.

What does the relationship mean for customers?

The public announcements point to a shared interest in enterprise digital transformation and stronger service integration. For customers, the relevant question is how that relationship supports delivery. SNS capabilities include:

  • SAP consulting and implementation across ERP, HR, procurement, analytics, and related processes.
  • Systems integration and enterprise application services.
  • AI and data analytics through practical, governed use cases.
  • Custom software development and digital platforms.
  • Cloud, data management, and ongoing application support.
  • Locally developed SNAM solutions integrated with SAP SuccessFactors.

Specific customer offerings should be evaluated using current project scope and official product information. The investment relationship does not mean that every Elm and SNS service is automatically combined. It does provide a strategic foundation for collaboration and integrated value where the companies choose to bring their capabilities together.

How the relationship supports Saudi digital transformation

Saudi Vision 2030 places strong emphasis on digital government, private-sector growth, local capability, innovation, and a diversified economy. Investment in a Saudi enterprise technology company supports those themes by helping expand local delivery capacity, intellectual property, specialized employment, and the ability to execute complex transformation programs inside the Kingdom.

SNS works across public and private sectors and connects global enterprise platforms with Saudi delivery and locally developed solutions. That combination supports organizations that need both proven technology and an implementation model grounded in the regional operating environment.

Is SNS part of Elm?

The most precise answer is that Elm is a 40 percent shareholder in SNS based on Elm's 2025 annual report. Elm is not reported as the sole owner. The annual report classifies the investment as a joint venture after joint control over key decisions was established.
